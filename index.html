Donate Computing Hardware

Useful computers and components can keep contributing long after their first use. We configure donated systems for Folding@home, monitor them for stability, and turn CPU/GPU capacity into completed research work units. We currently coordinate local donations in the Chicago, Milwaukee, and Northern Indiana areas.

  • Modern NVIDIA GPUs, especially RTX 3060 or newer, have the highest throughput
  • Stable desktops and workstations with adequate cooling and power delivery
  • CPU-only systems may still be useful depending on age, condition, and power draw
  • Drives can be wiped before pickup, or we can securely erase them before reuse
